I'm a second-year Physics PhD student at the University of Florida, advised by Dr. Richard Hennig. I build generative machine-learning models to discover new superconductors.

**Research interests:** generative models, machine learning for materials discovery, superconductivity, high-throughput computational screening.

Before UF, I earned a B.S. in Physics and a B.A. in Mathematics from Carthage College. My undergraduate research covered gravitational-wave noise characterization with LIGO (published in *Classical and Quantum Gravity*), quantum information theory at Penn State, and analytic correlation functions for materials and cosmology. Details are on my [Research](/portfolio/) page.

I'm originally from Minnesota, and outside of research I enjoy lifting, listening to and playing music, and backpacking.

You can also find me on my [UF page](https://sites.google.com/view/andrew-valentini/about).
